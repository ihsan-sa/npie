I also added the Closed line for #36 and pointed the run-report goal at #36.
Decided: The check asks whether a constant's value is disclosed, not whether its name is: `derived_from` must change when the constant changes. That keeps the operator-facing wording plain ("+/-5 %" instead of "TOL_REG") and still catches a hidden number. Disclosure isn't a design input, though. The alive floor and the diode band are still procgen rules that no design file gives. If the owner reads "traces to the board's design files" strictly, the next goal is moving them into constraints `bringup{}` like the short floors.

A later goal could promote them to constants so this check covers them too. That's mechanical, and none of them is hidden today: each one is already in its step's wording.
