Decided: I made signals reuse the Ctrl-C path instead of adding a new status, so the report, `resume`'s refusal and `exit_code` need no change. The handlers live in `npie_run.main` and not in `runner`, so a library caller (tests, a future skill script) keeps its own signal handling. The exit codes follow the shell's 128 + signal convention.
