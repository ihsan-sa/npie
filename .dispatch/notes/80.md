Decided: Read-back tolerance is 10 mV and 10 mA (`PSU_READBACK_V`/`_A` in instruments, not procgen, since they set no limit). A supply's setting resolution is at worst that coarse, and it is well inside any rail tolerance. Refuse a wrong setpoint before `OUTP ON`, not after, so a clamped or ignored setting never powers the board. I didn't add a measured-voltage check after power-on. That is a reading against the board's load, which the procedure's own measure steps already cover.
Learned: `OUTP?` answers `1`/`0` on SCPI-99 supplies, and `ON`/`OFF` on some (Siglent SPD among them, from memory, unverified), so both are accepted. `INST:NSEL` is still sent unconditionally, and a single-channel supply that rejects it would now show up as a read-back error rather than silently.
